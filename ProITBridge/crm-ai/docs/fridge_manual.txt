FROSTKING REFRIGERATOR — OWNER'S MANUAL
Models: FK-260L, FK-340L, FK-450L (Frost-Free Series)
=============================================================

SECTION 1: PRODUCT OVERVIEW
-----------------------------
FrostKing Frost-Free Refrigerators feature multi-airflow technology for uniform cooling.
Capacity: 260L, 340L, 450L
Energy Rating: 3-Star / 5-Star BEE rated
Compressor: Digital Inverter with 10-year warranty
Cooling Type: Frost-Free (No manual defrosting required)
Refrigerant: R-600a (eco-friendly)

SECTION 2: INSTALLATION
-------------------------
- Allow 10 cm clearance on sides and 15 cm at the back
- Place on level floor; adjust leveling feet if needed
- Keep away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and gas stoves
- After transport: wait 4 hours before switching on (compressor oil settles)
- Ambient temperature range: 10°C to 43°C (SN-T class)

SECTION 3: TEMPERATURE GUIDE
------------------------------
Freezer: -18°C (recommended for long-term storage)
  - Ideal for: Ice cream, meat, fish, frozen vegetables
Refrigerator: 3°C–5°C (recommended)
  - Ideal for: Dairy, cooked food, beverages, fruits/vegetables

Temperature Zones:
- Top shelf: 3°C–4°C → Dairy, drinks, leftovers
- Middle shelf: 4°C–5°C → Cooked food, eggs
- Crisper drawer: 8°C–10°C (high humidity) → Vegetables, fruits
- Door shelves: 5°C–8°C → Condiments, juices, butter

SECTION 4: CONTROLS
----------------------
Digital Display Panel:
- Fridge Temp: Use +/- buttons. Range 1°C–7°C.
- Freezer Temp: Use +/- buttons. Range -14°C to -22°C.
- Express Freeze: Super-cools freezer for 2.5 hours before reverting to set temp.
- Holiday Mode: Maintains 15°C in fridge (saves energy when away for weeks).
- Child Lock: Hold "Lock" for 3 seconds to disable panel.
- Power Cool: Rapid cooling for fresh groceries.

SECTION 5: ICE MAKER (FK-450L only)
--------------------------------------
- Fill water reservoir or connect to water line
- Ice production: ~100 ice cubes per day
- Ice full sensor: stops production when bin is full
- Clean ice bin monthly with warm water

SECTION 6: STORAGE TIPS
--------------------------
- Do NOT overfill; leave space for air circulation
- Store raw meat in sealed containers on the lowest shelf
- Keep strong-smelling foods covered
- Do NOT store hot food directly (allow to cool to room temp first)
- Fruits and vegetables: store separately (fruits emit ethylene gas)
- Eggs: store in main compartment (not door) for stability
- Maximum storage: cooked food 3–4 days, raw meat 1–2 days

SECTION 7: MAINTENANCE
------------------------
Monthly:
  - Clean door gaskets with warm soapy water; dry thoroughly
  - Check drain hole at back of fridge (clear any debris)
  - Wipe interior with baking soda solution (1 tsp per litre water)

Every 6 months:
  - Clean condenser coils at the back (vacuum or brush)
  - Check and replace water filter (if applicable)
  - Inspect door seal for tears or gaps

Annual:
  - Professional check of refrigerant level and compressor performance
  - Thermostat calibration

SECTION 8: DEFROST (Frost-Free models are automatic)
------------------------------------------------------
FK-260L / FK-340L / FK-450L use automatic frost-free system.
Defrost cycle runs every 6–8 hours for 20–30 minutes.
During defrost: compressor stops, fans stop, small temp rise is NORMAL.
Water drains automatically through drain hole to evaporation tray at bottom.

SECTION 9: ALARM CODES
------------------------
DOOR OPEN: Door open >2 minutes → Close door
HIGH TEMP: Temperature above safe level → Check door seal; call service if persists
PC ER: PCB/sensor error → Unplug 10 min, restart; call service if persists
dF Er: Defrost sensor error → Service required
FN Er: Fan motor error → Service required

SECTION 10: POWER FAILURE GUIDELINES
---------------------------------------
Up to 4 hours: Food remains safe if door is NOT opened
4–8 hours: Check food before consuming; ice cream may soften
Over 8 hours: Check meat and dairy carefully; discard if smell is off
Tip: Place a thermometer inside to monitor temperature
