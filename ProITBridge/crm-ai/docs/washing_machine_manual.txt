SECTION 1: PRODUCT OVERVIEW
-----------------------------
WashPro Washing Machines deliver efficient fabric care with intelligent wash programs.
Capacity: 6.5kg (Front Load), 7.5kg / 8kg (Top Load)
Energy: 5-Star rated
Motor: Brushless Inverter Motor (10-year warranty)
Spin Speed: Up to 1400 RPM (Front Load), 740 RPM (Top Load)

SECTION 2: INSTALLATION
-------------------------
- Place on a firm, level surface. Use leveling feet to eliminate vibration.
- Water inlet pressure: 0.3–1.0 MPa
- Drain hose height: 60–90 cm from floor (top load); wall drain recommended
- Electrical: Dedicated 15A earthed socket; do NOT use extension cord
- Keep at least 5 cm from walls and other appliances
- Remove transit bolts (3–4 bolts at back) before first use — CRITICAL!

SECTION 3: DETERGENT GUIDE
-----------------------------
Front Load (WP-6.5F):
  - Use ONLY HE (High Efficiency) low-foam detergent
  - Drawer: Compartment I = Pre-wash, II = Main wash, III = Fabric softener
  - Liquid: Use liquid detergent compartment (II slot)
  - Recommended dose: 40–60ml for normal load, 70–80ml for heavily soiled

Top Load (WP-7.5T / WP-8T):
  - Standard or HE detergent (both work)
  - Add detergent before clothes, or use dispenser drawer
  - Avoid overloading detergent — excess foam reduces wash quality

SECTION 4: WASH PROGRAMS
--------------------------
Cotton / Normal: Full wash for cotton garments (60°C capable)
Synthetic / Delicate: Gentle wash for synthetic, blends
Quick Wash (15 min): Lightly soiled clothes, small loads
Woollen: Gentle tumble, cold water, low spin
Heavy Duty: Work clothes, jeans, heavily soiled
Baby Care: Hot wash + extra rinse for baby clothes
Dark Care: Cold wash to preserve dark fabric colors
Soak + Wash: 20-minute pre-soak for tough stains
Drum Clean: Self-cleaning cycle (run empty, no detergent, monthly)
Rinse + Spin: Rinse and spin only (add rinse cycle manually)
Spin Only: Spin to remove excess water after soaking

SECTION 5: TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
-----------------------------------
- Sort clothes: whites, lights, darks, delicates separately
- Check care labels for maximum wash temperature
- Do NOT overload: drum should be 3/4 full maximum
- Zip up zippers; turn dark clothes inside out
- Pre-treat tough stains before washing
- Use fabric softener in final rinse for softness
- Do NOT wash heavily soiled items with lightly soiled (cross-contamination)
- Check pockets before loading (coins can damage drum)

SECTION 6: SPIN SPEED GUIDE
------------------------------
400 RPM: Delicates, silk, lace
600 RPM: Woollens, synthetics
800–1000 RPM: Casuals, T-shirts
1200–1400 RPM: Cotton, towels, jeans

SECTION 7: ERROR CODES
------------------------
E1 / F1: Water inlet error → Check tap is open; check inlet hose kink
E2 / F2: Drainage error → Check drain hose not kinked; check filter
E3 / F3: Door lock error → Ensure door is firmly closed; restart
E4 / F4: Overflow protection → Check water inlet valve
E5 / F5: Motor error → Check load distribution; service required
E6 / F6: Heating element error → Service required
E7 / F7: Imbalance detected → Redistribute clothes evenly
UNB: Imbalance during spin → Open and redistribute load

SECTION 8: MAINTENANCE
------------------------
Weekly:
  - Wipe door seal/gasket (front load: check for lint/debris in folds)
  - Leave door ajar after wash to prevent mold

Monthly:
  - Run Drum Clean cycle (empty drum, no detergent, 90°C)
  - Clean lint filter (top load: inside drum near top)
  - Clean pump filter (front load: lower front panel, small door)
  - Wipe detergent drawer with warm water

Every 6 months:
  - Clean inlet filter mesh (unscrew hose from tap end)
  - Check drain hose for blockage
  - Professional service recommended
