SECTION 1: PRODUCT OVERVIEW
-----------------------------
MicroChef Microwave Ovens combine microwave, grill, and convection for versatile cooking.
Capacity: 20L (Solo), 28L (Convection), 32L (Grill + Convection)
Power: 700W / 900W / 1000W microwave; 1200W grill; 2200W convection
Frequency: 2450 MHz
Turntable: 25.5 cm (Solo/Conv), 31.5 cm (Grill+Conv)

SECTION 2: COOKWARE GUIDE
---------------------------
SAFE for microwave:
  - Glass, Ceramic, Microwave-safe plastic (labeled MW safe)
  - Paper towel, parchment paper (for covering)
  - Borosilicate glass (Pyrex)

NOT SAFE for microwave:
  - Metals, foil, metallic crockery
  - Sealed containers (pressure buildup)
  - Styrofoam (unless specifically marked microwave-safe)

Safe for GRILL mode (MC-28C, MC-32G):
  - Metal rack provided with oven
  - Grill-safe glass, ceramic

Safe for CONVECTION mode:
  - Metal baking trays, aluminum foil dishes
  - All oven-safe cookware

SECTION 3: COOKING MODES
--------------------------
SOLO (MC-20S): Microwave only — reheating, defrosting, basic cooking
MICROWAVE (all models): Power levels 10%–100%. 
  - 100%: Boiling, reheating liquids
  - 70%: Cooking fish, vegetables
  - 50%: Cooking meat, casseroles
  - 30%: Defrosting
  - 10%: Keeping warm

GRILL (MC-28C, MC-32G): Browning and crisping. Use metal rack.
CONVECTION (MC-28C, MC-32G): Preheat to 180°C–200°C. Baking, roasting.
COMBINATION modes:
  - Micro + Grill: Cook through + brown simultaneously
  - Micro + Convection: Faster baking

SECTION 5: DEFROST GUIDE
--------------------------
By Weight: Enter weight → auto calculates time
By Time: Enter time at 30% power
After defrosting: Rest food 5 minutes before cooking
Do NOT defrost whole large birds (uneven defrosting risk)
Tips:
  - Place frozen food on microwave-safe plate
  - Flip/rearrange halfway through defrost cycle
  - Cook immediately after defrosting

SECTION 6: CLEANING GUIDE
----------------------------
Daily / After each use:
  - Wipe interior walls with damp cloth while warm (easier to clean)
  - Clean turntable plate and ring: remove and wash with soap & water

Weekly:
  - Deep clean interior: warm water + 1 tsp baking soda; microwave 3 min; wipe down
  - Clean door seals with damp cloth (do NOT use abrasive scrubbers)
  - Wipe exterior with damp cloth

Important:
  - Never use metal scrubbers inside
  - Do NOT spray cleaners directly inside
  - Keep the air vents on sides and back clear of obstruction

SECTION 7: ERROR CODES
------------------------
E-1: Temperature sensor error → Power off, cool 15 min, restart; service if persists
E-2: Humidity sensor error → Service required
E-3: Door sensor error → Check door is fully closed; service if persists
E-4: Magnetron overheating → Allow 15 min cool down; check ventilation space
E-5: Turntable motor error → Check turntable is seated properly on ring
E-6: Fan motor error → Service required
H-97: Magnetron fault → Service required (do NOT attempt to repair yourself — radiation hazard)

SECTION 8: SAFETY WARNINGS
-----------------------------
⚠ Never run microwave when empty — can damage magnetron
⚠ Never use sealed containers — pressure buildup explosion risk
⚠ Never heat breast milk in microwave — uneven heating burns baby
⚠ Never deep fry in microwave
⚠ Eggs in shell MUST be pierced before microwaving
⚠ If door seal is damaged, stop using and call service immediately
⚠ Do NOT attempt to repair magnetron — radiation hazard
