APPLIANCE INSTALLATION GUIDE — ALL PRODUCTS
ProITBridge Appliance Company

SECTION 1: AC INSTALLATION
-----------------------------
Step 1: Site Survey
  - Confirm wall strength for outdoor unit (min 15kg load capacity)
  - Identify ideal indoor unit position: central wall, 7–8 ft high, away from direct sunlight
  - Measure copper pipe route; keep under 15 meters

Step 2: Indoor Unit Mounting
  - Mark and drill wall plate holes (use provided template)
  - Insert wall anchors; mount wall plate; level with spirit level
  - Cut 70mm hole for pipe/cable exit at slight downward angle (for drainage)
  - Hang indoor unit on wall plate; click into position

Step 3: Outdoor Unit Placement
  - Mount on bracket (included) or ground stand
  - Ensure 30 cm clearance on all sides
  - Secure anti-vibration pads under unit

Step 4: Refrigerant Piping
  - Use provided copper pipe set (pre-insulated)
  - Flare pipe ends using flaring tool
  - Connect to indoor and outdoor units; torque to spec (24–26 Nm for 1/4" pipe)
  - Wrap insulation tape on all exposed pipe joints

Step 5: Electrical Connection
  - Run 4-core cable between indoor and outdoor units
  - Connect to terminal block per wiring diagram (colors: L, N, Ground, Signal)
  - Ensure dedicated circuit breaker is installed

Step 6: Vacuum and Gas Check
  - Technician must vacuum the line set for 30+ minutes
  - Check for pressure drop (indicates leak)
  - Open service valve to release refrigerant from outdoor unit

Step 7: Test Run
  - Power on; run in Cool mode for 10 minutes
  - Check indoor air temperature drop (should be 8–12°C below room temp)
  - Check for unusual noise or vibration

SECTION 2: REFRIGERATOR INSTALLATION
---------------------------------------
Step 1: Unboxing
  - Remove all packaging, foam inserts, and tape
  - Remove transit bolts from compressor at back (labeled RED)
  - Keep transit bolts for future transport

Step 2: Positioning
  - Allow 10 cm side clearance, 15 cm rear clearance, 5 cm top clearance
  - Level using adjustable front feet (turn clockwise to raise)
  - Ensure slightly back-tilted (~1 cm) so door swings closed automatically

Step 3: Wait before Switching On
  - If transported horizontally: wait 24 hours
  - If transported upright: wait 4 hours
  - Reason: Compressor oil must settle back

Step 4: First Use
  - Wipe interior with mild soap solution; dry completely
  - Set temperature to mid-range (3°C fridge, -18°C freezer)
  - Allow 4–6 hours to reach operating temperature before loading food

SECTION 3: WASHING MACHINE INSTALLATION
-----------------------------------------
Step 1: Remove Transit Bolts
  - Locate 3–4 transit bolts at the back (with red/yellow caps)
  - Remove ALL bolts using provided spanner
  - Keep bolts — required if machine needs to be moved
  - Cover holes with provided plastic plugs

Step 2: Leveling
  - Place on firm, level floor (not carpet)
  - Adjust leveling feet so all 4 corners are stable
  - Check with spirit level; max 1° inclination allowed
  - Tighten lock nuts on feet when level

Step 3: Water Connections
  - Connect inlet hose to tap (3/4" BSP fitting; hand-tighten + 1/2 turn wrench)
  - For hot+cold models: red hose = hot, blue = cold
  - Ensure rubber washer is seated inside coupling before connecting

Step 4: Drain Setup
  - Route drain hose to drain pipe or laundry tub
  - Height: 60–90 cm above floor level
  - Secure with drain hose clip (prevents siphoning)
  - Do NOT insert hose more than 15 cm into drain pipe

Step 5: First Wash
  - Run empty Drum Clean or Cotton 60°C cycle before first use
  - This removes any manufacturing residue
  - Check all connections for leaks during this cycle

SECTION 4: MICROWAVE INSTALLATION
------------------------------------
Step 1: Placement
  - Place on flat, stable surface
  - Allow 10 cm clearance on sides and 20 cm on top and rear (ventilation)
  - Do NOT install inside closed cabinet without ventilation

Step 2: Electrical
  - Plug into grounded 15A socket
  - Do NOT use extension cord or multi-socket adaptor

Step 3: Setup
  - Set clock: Press Clock → enter time → press Clock again
  - Place turntable ring and glass turntable plate
  - Do NOT operate without turntable

SECTION 5: TV INSTALLATION
-----------------------------
Wall Mounting:
  - Use VESA-compatible wall mount (VESA 200×200 for 43"; 400×400 for 55"/65")
  - Ensure wall can bear TV weight + mount (min 3× TV weight)
  - Mark stud locations; drill into studs for secure mounting
  - Use provided VESA bolt size (M6 × 20mm for most models)
  - Minimum viewing distance: 43" = 1.8m, 55" = 2.3m, 65" = 2.7m

Cable Management:
  - Use adhesive cable clips or in-wall cable conduit
  - Keep power and HDMI cables separated to reduce interference
