APPLIANCE WARRANTY POLICY
==========================

1. STANDARD WARRANTY COVERAGE
All appliances come with a manufacturer warranty:
- Air Conditioners: 2 years (compressor: 5 years)
- Refrigerators: 2 years (compressor: 5 years)
- Washing Machines: 2 years (motor: 5 years)
- Televisions: 1 year
- Microwaves: 1 year
- Dishwashers: 2 years
- Water Purifiers: 1 year (membrane: 2 years)
- Air Purifiers: 1 year

2. WARRANTY CLAIM PROCESS
Step 1: Customer raises a warranty claim via chat, email, or phone.
Step 2: System verifies purchase date and warranty status.
Step 3: Technician is dispatched within 24-48 hours for in-home inspection.
Step 4: If defect is covered, repair or replacement is done free of charge.
Step 5: If defect is not covered, customer is informed with repair cost estimate.

3. WARRANTY EXCLUSIONS
The warranty does NOT cover:
- Physical damage (drops, cracks, dents)
- Water damage or flooding
- Damage from power surges (use AVR/stabilizer recommended)
- Unauthorized repair attempts
- Consumables (filters, bulbs, batteries)
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from improper installation

4. EXTENDED WARRANTY
Customers can purchase extended warranty plans:
- 1-year extension: ₹999–₹2,499 depending on product
- 2-year extension: ₹1,799–₹4,999 depending on product
Extended warranty must be purchased within 12 months of original purchase.

5. WARRANTY TRANSFER
Warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser.

6. CLAIM APPROVAL AUTHORITY
- Claims under ₹5,000: Auto-approved by system
- Claims ₹5,000–₹20,000: Approved by support team lead
- Claims above ₹20,000: Requires manager approval (human-in-the-loop)
- Refund requests: Always require human review
