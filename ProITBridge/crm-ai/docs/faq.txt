FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
==================================

Q: How do I check my warranty status?
A: Share your Customer ID or Invoice Number in the chat. Our system will instantly check your warranty expiry date.

Q: How long does repair take?
A: Most repairs are completed within 1-3 business days. Complex repairs (compressor replacement, board repair) may take up to 7 days.

Q: What are the service charges after warranty expires?
A: Diagnostic visit: ₹500 (adjusted against repair cost). Labor charges: ₹800-₹2,500 depending on complexity. Parts charged at MRP.

Q: Can I get a replacement instead of repair?
A: Replacement is offered if: (1) the product fails 3+ times within warranty, or (2) the repair cost exceeds 70% of product value.

Q: How do I schedule a technician visit?
A: You can schedule through chat, our website, or call 1800-XXX-XXXX. Available Monday-Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM.

Q: My product was delivered damaged. What do I do?
A: Do not accept the delivery if visibly damaged. If damage is noticed after opening, contact us within 48 hours with photos. We will arrange replacement pickup and delivery.

Q: How do I cancel a repair appointment?
A: Contact us at least 4 hours before the appointment. Cancellations within 2 hours may incur a ₹200 rescheduling fee.

Q: Is there a demo facility available?
A: Yes! Visit any of our experience centers or request an in-home demo for products above ₹25,000.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept: Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), EMI (no-cost EMI available on select cards), Cash on Delivery (up to ₹25,000).

Q: How do I escalate an unresolved complaint?
A: If your complaint is not resolved within 5 business days, it is automatically escalated to our senior team. You can also request escalation via chat at any time.

Q: Do you offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC)?
A: Yes! AMC plans start at ₹1,200/year and include 2 free services, priority technician dispatch, and 20% discount on parts.

Q: What is the energy rating system?
A: Indian appliances use BEE (Bureau of Energy Efficiency) star ratings 1-5. 5-star products are most energy efficient and save 30-40% on electricity bills.
