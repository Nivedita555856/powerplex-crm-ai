RETURN & REFUND POLICY
=======================

1. RETURN WINDOW
- 7 days: Damaged or defective product on delivery
- 30 days: Product does not match description
- No return: After 30 days (warranty service applies instead)

2. REFUND PROCESS
Step 1: Customer requests refund via support ticket.
Step 2: Support team reviews request within 24 hours.
Step 3: Technician inspects the product at customer location.
Step 4: If eligible, pickup is scheduled within 48 hours.
Step 5: Refund is processed within 5-7 business days to original payment method.

3. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
ELIGIBLE FOR FULL REFUND:
- Product dead on arrival (DOA)
- Product does not match specifications
- Wrong product delivered
- Product damaged during delivery

ELIGIBLE FOR PARTIAL REFUND (30-50%):
- Product used but has persistent unresolvable defect
- Customer dissatisfied after 2+ repair attempts

NOT ELIGIBLE FOR REFUND:
- Product damaged by customer misuse
- Return request after 30 days
- Consumables (filters, remotes, cables)
- Products purchased during clearance sale

4. REFUND AMOUNTS
- Full refund: 100% of purchase price
- Partial refund: 30-70% based on usage and defect severity
- Exchange: Replace with same model or upgrade (customer pays difference)

5. APPROVAL AUTHORITY
- Refunds under ₹10,000: Support team can approve
- Refunds ₹10,000–₹50,000: Requires team lead approval
- Refunds above ₹50,000: Requires manager + accounts approval
- All refunds require human-in-the-loop review before processing
