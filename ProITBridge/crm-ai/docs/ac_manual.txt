SECTION 2: INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------
- Outdoor unit: Min 30 cm clearance on all sides; avoid direct sunlight
- Indoor unit: Mount 7–8 feet high; avoid heat sources nearby
- Copper pipe length: Max 15 meters between units
- Electrical: Dedicated 15A/20A circuit; stabilizer recommended in areas with voltage fluctuation
- Drainage: 15-degree downward slope for drain pipe; min 1.5-inch diameter

SECTION 3: OPERATING MODES
----------------------------
COOL Mode: Standard cooling. Set temperature 16°C–30°C.
FAN Mode: Air circulation only, no cooling.
DRY Mode: Dehumidification. Reduces humidity without aggressive cooling.
AUTO Mode: Automatically switches between cool/heat based on room temp.
HEAT Mode (CB-2T, CB-2.5T only): Heating in winter. Works up to -7°C ambient.
SLEEP Mode: Gradually raises temp by 1°C/hr for comfort sleeping.
TURBO Mode: Maximum cooling for first 30 minutes.

SECTION 6: MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
----------------------------------
Every 2 weeks:
  - Clean/rinse the air filter (front panel)
  - Wipe indoor unit with dry cloth

Every 3 months:
  - Deep clean evaporator coil (call service)
  - Check drain pipe for blockage
  - Check remote battery

Every 6 months (Annual Service):
  - Full service: gas pressure check, coil cleaning, drainage flush
  - Professional cleaning recommended before summer season
  - Check electrical connections and capacitors

SECTION 7: FILTER CLEANING STEPS
-----------------------------------
1. Switch off AC and unplug from power.
2. Lift the front panel cover upward.
3. Slide out the mesh filters (left and right).
4. Wash under running water; use mild soap if greasy.
5. Dry completely in shade (do NOT use direct sunlight/dryer).
6. Reinsert filters; press front panel closed.
7. Reset filter-clean indicator: hold "Auto" button 3 seconds.

SECTION 8: ERROR CODES
------------------------
E1  — Indoor room sensor fault → Replace thermistor
E2  — Evaporator coil sensor fault → Service required
E3  — High pressure protection → Check refrigerant, clean filter
E4  — Low pressure / gas leak → Gas top-up required (certified technician)
E5  — Compressor overload → Check voltage; rest unit 30 minutes
E6  — Communication error (indoor↔outdoor) → Check wiring
E7  — Outdoor ambient sensor fault → Service required
F1  — Refrigerant leak detected → Evacuate and call service immediately
H1  — Defrost mode active (normal in heat mode)
P1  — Power fluctuation protection → Check power supply

SECTION 9: ENERGY SAVING TIPS
--------------------------------
- Set temperature to 24°C–26°C for optimal efficiency
- Use timer to avoid running AC when not needed
- Close windows and doors while AC is running
- Clean filters every 2 weeks
- Use ceiling fan alongside AC to spread cooling
- Draw curtains/blinds during peak afternoon heat
