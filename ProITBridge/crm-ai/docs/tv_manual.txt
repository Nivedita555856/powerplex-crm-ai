SECTION 3: INITIAL SETUP
--------------------------
1. Unbox and attach stand (or wall mount). See separate stand assembly guide.
2. Connect power cable. Do NOT switch on yet.
3. Connect antenna/cable/HDTV cable to RF IN port.
4. Power on. Follow on-screen setup wizard.
5. Select language → connect to WiFi → sign in with Google account.
6. Android TV home screen will appear.
7. Download desired streaming apps from Google Play Store.

SECTION 7: CONNECTIVITY TROUBLESHOOTING
-----------------------------------------
WiFi not connecting:
  - Ensure TV is within range of router
  - Forget network and reconnect
  - Check router frequency (use 5GHz for faster speeds)

No picture on HDMI:
  - Check HDMI cable connection on both ends
  - Try different HDMI port
  - Check source device is outputting signal
  - Ensure HDMI cable supports 4K (High Speed HDMI)

Bluetooth pairing:
  - Go to Settings > Remote & Accessories > Add Accessory
  - Put device in pairing mode
  - Select from list

SECTION 8: ERROR CODES / INDICATORS
--------------------------------------
Red LED blinking (2x): Power supply issue → Unplug 30 sec, reconnect
Red LED blinking (4x): Panel backlight fault → Service required
Red LED blinking (6x): Main board fault → Service required
No signal: Check antenna/cable connection and input source
App not loading: Clear app cache in Settings > Apps > [App] > Clear Cache

SECTION 9: MAINTENANCE
------------------------
- Clean screen with microfiber cloth (dry or slightly damp with distilled water)
- Do NOT use glass cleaner, alcohol, or abrasive materials on screen
- Dust vents at back monthly with soft brush
- Update software: Settings > Device Preferences > About > System Update
- Factory reset: Settings > Device Preferences > Reset (last resort)

SECTION 10: GAMING MODE
-------------------------
Enable: Settings > Picture > Game Mode
- Input lag reduced to <10ms
- VRR (Variable Refresh Rate) enabled (for compatible consoles)
- ALLM (Auto Low Latency Mode) automatically activates when console detected
- Best HDMI port for gaming: HDMI 1 (supports 120Hz on VX-55S/65S)
